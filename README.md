Monster Job Interview is a narrative-driven tavern management game where you interview monsters applying for a place in your tavern.

Every morning, you'll review the night's reservations, and decide which monsters to accept based on their personalities, traits, and the customers expected to visit. Your choices determine whether the night ends in profit, harmony, or complete chaos.

Balance monster and human relationships, manage your tavern's reputation, and climb the ranks until even the king takes notice.

Features:

*Dialogue with options
*Monsters with different traits
*World events indicating wether monsters are lying or not
*Relationships between monsters and NPCs
