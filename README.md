<p align="center">

## Welcome to the Tavern

Running a tavern is hard enough. Running one for monsters? That's another story.

Every morning, a fresh stack of reservations awaits. You'll interview monster applicants, examine their personalities and traits, and decide who gets a place in your tavern.

But there's more to consider than a monster's first impression. The customers expected that night, strange world events, and relationships between monsters can all influence how things unfold.

Make the right choices and your tavern will thrive. Make the wrong ones, and you might end up with a night nobody forgets.

##  Features

| | |
|:---|:---|
|  **Dialogue Choices** | Interview applicants through dialogue and make decisions that shape their stories. |
|  **Unique Monster Traits** | Discover different personalities and traits that influence who belongs in your tavern. |
|  **World Events** | Pay attention to events that can reveal whether monsters are telling the truth. |
|  **Relationships** | Navigate relationships between monsters and other NPCs. |
|  **Tavern Management** | Balance profit, reputation, and the delicate relationship between humans and monsters. |
|  **Rise Through the Ranks** | Build your reputation until even the king takes notice. |

##  A Day at the Tavern

1. **Review Reservations** — Find out which customers are expected that night.
2. **Interview Applicants** — Get to know the monsters looking for a place in your tavern.
3. **Read Between the Lines** — Use traits, relationships, and world events to uncover the truth.
4. **Make Your Choices** — Decide who gets accepted.
5. **Face the Consequences** — See how your decisions affect the night's outcome.
